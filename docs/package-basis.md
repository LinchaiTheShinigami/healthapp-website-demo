## Pricing Note

The two price points per tier reflect the collection route:

- `Home kit` includes the capillary kit workflow
- `Clinic appointment` includes the clinic venous draw route

The Sussex PDF also lists:

- home kit charge: `GBP 11.50`
- clinic phlebotomy appointment: `GBP 25`

Ayuta’s current tier pricing is a commercial demo price band built on that underlying menu, not a direct pass-through invoice of the B2B component totals.
