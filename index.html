Plain English

Common terms explained before people hit the booking flow

What is a panel?

A panel is simply a group of biomarkers bundled together around one theme such as energy, hormones, or metabolic health.

What is a biomarker?

A biomarker is a measurable value in your blood, like ferritin, vitamin D, HbA1c, or cholesterol, that helps explain what is happening in your body.

What is a certified testing lab?

It means the sample is processed by an accredited laboratory partner rather than by Ayuta directly, helping maintain testing quality and traceability.

FAQs

Questions people usually ask before buying

Do you offer home kits, lab visits, or both?

The updated demo shows both: at-home kits for convenience and partner lab visits for people who prefer an in-person draw.

How quickly do results come back?

Partner lab appointments can return results in roughly 24 to 72 hours after the sample is processed, while home kits add delivery and return time.

What does a panel include?

Each panel is a bundle of biomarkers around a specific health question. The booking page now shows the exact markers and a short explanation of why each matters.

Can I see where labs are located before ordering?

Yes. The home page now lists the current UK demo coverage so people can judge convenience immediately.

Can I speak to someone before ordering?

Yes. The contact page now includes a consultation option for people who want help choosing a test or understanding the process.
