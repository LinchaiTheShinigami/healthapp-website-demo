Partnership Models

Two ways to start.

Start with a simple member opt-in, then add aggregate insight once volume and consent are in place.

01

Individual Opt-In

A member buys their own Ayuta package and opts in to share selected results with their coach or gym programme. Opting in unlocks a member discount. Members who prefer privacy can still test anonymously while contributing aggregated data to programme development.

Member pays directly Discount for opting in Consent-led data sharing Anonymous option available
02

Aggregated Anonymous Insight

Members test independently and contribute anonymised data to a programme-level view. Coaches see broad trends across the member base — recovery patterns, deficiency profiles, training readiness — without ever accessing individual results.

Anonymous group patterns Programme-level insight No individual disclosure Useful for programme design
