Service Principles

The product direction is straightforward.

Reduce noise, keep the route explicit, and make the account experience feel like part of the product rather than an afterthought.

Principle 1

Credibility should come from clarity, not volume.

Panels, pricing, and collection routes should all read quickly without long paragraphs or filler reassurance.

Principle 2

Mobile and laptop should still feel like the same product.

The layout is designed to compress cleanly rather than hiding structure or exploding into long stacks on smaller screens.

Principle 3

Payments stay lean first, then get verified properly.

The current Stripe Payment Link demo is intentionally thin while the Firebase-backed confirmation layer is built next.
