Service Principles

We built Ayuta around three commitments.

Blood testing should be straightforward to choose, convenient to complete, and easy to follow up on. Those three things shaped every decision.

Principle 1

Understand what you are buying before you commit.

Package contents, pricing, and collection options are all visible before checkout. No buried small print, no surprises at the door.

Principle 2

Test from wherever makes sense for you.

Home finger-prick kits and partner clinic venous draws are both first-class options. The choice stays yours throughout the booking flow.

Principle 3

Results should explain themselves.

Your dashboard shows what each marker means, why it matters for your goals, and how it has changed over time. Context comes with the numbers.
