About

Ayuta Health exists to make preventive testing easier to understand and easier to act on

We are building a calmer, more human blood-testing experience for people who want clarity on their health without being forced to decode clinical jargon, confusing panel names, or flat PDF reports.

Why this matters

Many people know they should check things like iron, vitamin D, cholesterol, or glucose, but they do not know which test to pick or what the result means afterwards.

Ayuta is designed to reduce that friction by making selection, collection, payment, and results feel like one connected flow instead of four disconnected steps.

What Ayuta does for customers

  • Helps people choose a suitable test using goals and guided recommendations.
  • Makes the collection model explicit with both home-kit and partner-lab options.
  • Shows biomarkers and plain-English explanations before checkout.
  • Presents result data in a chart-led dashboard instead of only a static report.
