Ordering

Foundation covers the essentials — a core panel of biomarkers including full blood count, liver and kidney function, thyroid, key vitamins, and cholesterol. A solid baseline for anyone starting out.

Performance expands into hormonal health, inflammation markers, and metabolic indicators — designed for people who train regularly or want a more complete picture of what's limiting or supporting their performance.

Elite is our most comprehensive panel. It includes everything in Performance plus advanced cardiovascular risk markers, detailed hormonal profiling, and biomarkers associated with longevity. Includes longitudinal dashboard tracking and a quarterly trend review.

Both options use the same laboratory and produce identical results. The choice is about what's most convenient for you.

The home kit is a finger-prick collection you do yourself, posted back in a prepaid envelope. Most people find it straightforward and appreciate not having to travel. It suits Foundation and Performance panels well.

A clinic appointment involves a venous blood draw by a trained phlebotomist — the same method used by private GPs. It's ideal if you'd prefer not to collect a sample yourself, or if you're ordering the Elite panel, which requires a larger sample volume. Clinic appointments are available in central London.

Your results

Results are typically released 3–5 working days after the laboratory receives your sample. You'll get an email notification the moment they're available on your dashboard.

The timeline runs: you post your sample → lab receives it (next day for tracked) → processing takes 1–2 days → doctor review → results released. Most people see results within a week of posting.

Your results come with clinical context for each biomarker — what it measures, where you sit within a reference range, and what can move it. This is designed to be meaningful whether you read it alone, with a PT, or alongside your GP.

You can download a formatted summary report directly from your dashboard to share with your doctor, trainer, or nutritionist. No translation needed — it's written to make sense to a health professional immediately.

Ayuta results are a data point, not a diagnosis. If a result is significantly outside range, always discuss it with a qualified clinician before making any changes to medication, supplementation, or training.

We'll contact you by email as soon as we hear from the lab — usually within 24 hours of them flagging it. We'll explain what happened and arrange a replacement kit at no charge.

Non-viable samples are most often caused by insufficient volume or delayed transit (e.g. posted on a Thursday or Friday). A repeat collection resolves this in the vast majority of cases.

If you'd prefer not to repeat and would like a refund instead, email us and we'll sort that for you.

Every time you retest, your new results are added to your dashboard as a data point on the same chart as your previous results. Over time — typically two or three tests in — you start to see direction of change, not just a snapshot.

This is where Ayuta gets genuinely useful. A single result tells you where you are. A trend line tells you whether a change in training, nutrition, or lifestyle is actually working at a physiological level.

Your GP or coach can see the same trend when you share your summary report — making conversations more productive and evidence-based.

Subscriptions & retesting

Choose a retesting interval — every 8, 12, or 16 weeks — and your kit is dispatched automatically at that cadence. Payment is taken from your card on file at the time of each dispatch.

Your dashboard builds a trend line with each test, so the longer you're on a subscription, the more valuable your data becomes. Most members notice meaningful pattern recognition emerging after three tests.

Log into your account, go to Billing, and manage your subscription from there. Alternatively, email us at hello@ayuta.co.uk and we'll action it for you.

To avoid being charged for the next cycle, cancel at least 7 days before your next scheduled dispatch date. You can see your next dispatch date in your account dashboard.

You can also pause for up to 12 weeks if you're travelling or want a break — without losing your trend history. Resume anytime from your account settings.

If we ever change subscription pricing, we'll give you 30 days' written notice before the new rate takes effect. You'll never be surprised by a charge you weren't expecting.
